Our story

A seed the forest gives once a year

The forest

Wild trees of the Venezuelan Amazon

The tonka tree, Dipteryx odorata, grows wild in the rainforest of southern Venezuela. Each season its fruit ripens and falls to the forest floor. Inside every fruit is a single long, wrinkled seed with a warm scent of vanilla, almond and cinnamon: the tonka bean.

The harvest

Gathered by hand, one fruit at a time

The harvest comes once a year. Local gatherers walk the forest and pick up the fallen fruit by hand. No trees are felled, and the forest stays standing. Each hard shell is cracked open to free its seed, and only whole, sound beans are kept.

Productos Ruffo

Dried, tested and sent abroad

At Productos Ruffo we dehydrate the beans slowly to a stable moisture level, which protects the seed and concentrates its aroma. Every lot is lab-tested, packed in kraft doypacks in Venezuela and exported to buyers abroad, from perfumers and chandlers to pastry kitchens.

8.17%Moisture, lab-verified

Dry enough to keep, rich enough to perfume a room. Our beans are tested by the Institute of Food Science and Technology of the Central University of Venezuela before they leave the country.
